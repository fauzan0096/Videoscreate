# voice.md — Sunnah Reels (fill the blanks, keep the rules)

## Who I am talking to
A Muslim in their 20s-40s scrolling Reels/Shorts, who knows the name of a sunnah but not its source, and is tired of
unsourced "hadith" in forwards.

## How the reels sound
Calm, short, no hype. One question, one answer, one source. Captions are plain English (Roman Urdu/Hindi optional on
the thumbnail only). No background music with instruments unless rights-free; nasheed/duff only.

## Words I never use
"viral", "shocking", "you won't believe", "secret", "100% sahih" (unless verified), "scholars say" (name the scholar),
"fatwa" (I am not giving fatawa).

## What I will NOT claim
- No hadith without book + number + a Shamela page I opened myself.
- No grading of my own. I quote a named muhaddith's grading and its book/page.
- No fixed reward numbers ("1000 hasanat") unless the text says exactly that.
- No translation presented as the Arabic; mark it "meaning".
- No ruling (halal/haram) — "ask a qualified scholar".
- If unverified, the reel stays DRAFT and is not posted.

## Verification checklist (before every post)
1. Arabic matn copied from Shamela page (book_id/page_id saved).
2. English meaning compared with a published translation.
3. Myth reels: grading source named in `ref`; title phrased as a question until then.
4. Ask a knowledgeable person to review the first 10 reels.

## My format
15-25 s, vertical, caption-only, ends with "Send this to someone who needs it.", channel name watermark, 1 reel/day.
